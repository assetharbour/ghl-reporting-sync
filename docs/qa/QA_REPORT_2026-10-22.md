## 6. Non-functional notes

* **Sheets quota:** about 5 Sheets requests per processed call and about 10 per audit cycle; Google's read quota is 60 per minute per user, so a burst of more than about 10 calls in a minute can hit 429. The store retries with backoff and the sweeper recovers anything left. Evonex's own ingestion shares the same quota.
* **Time per call:** 20 to 30 s of background work (5 s settle, Sheets latency 2 to 5 s per read). Acceptable because the webhook answers first.
* **Security:** no secrets in either repository (`.env*` ignored, scan clean); the sweep endpoint uses a constant-time secret comparison; the data endpoint is tested for access control and column leakage; unexpected webhook errors return a bare 500.

## 8. Residual risks and items not covered

1. **Live write and workflow trigger** (U1 to U5): proven only against fakes and read-only calls.
2. **Serverless behaviour of work after the response:** the write-back and the sweep use Starlette background tasks, the same mechanism the production sync already uses, but their completion on Vercel cannot be proven locally; the sweeper (every 15 minutes) is the safety net.
3. **Dependency advisories not fixed here:** Python: starlette 0.37.2 (8 advisories; the application does not use form parsing, static files, HTTPEndpoint, and the Host-header issue cannot forge a signature) and python-dotenv 1.0.1 (`set_key` symlink issue, not used). JavaScript: react-router 6.30.6 (open redirect via backslash in links, SSR hydration; the app is client-only with fixed internal links). Upgrading FastAPI / Starlette and moving to React Router 7 are separate changes with their own regression risk; recommended after go-live.
4. **Browsers:** tested in one Chromium-based browser only (no Firefox, Safari or Edge, no real screen-reader session, no visual-regression baseline).
5. **Data not yet representative:** the Appointments, Flags and Locks tabs do not exist yet; Fact Find Outcome is empty everywhere, so the full-fact-find and completion conversions are n/a until staff fill it.
6. **Open product items:** opportunities already at the new "Post Appointment" stage have a blank stage in the Leads sheet until the stage is mapped (`629e80ae-4f0f-4334-91ae-c677d3c11648`, and the "Client Contacted" rename); two advisor-name options (Carl Gladman, Anthony Fox) have had no appointments, which skews the round-robin share.
7. **Throughput limit:** more than roughly 10 call-ends per minute may exceed the Sheets read quota; a database or queue would be the next step if call volume grows.
